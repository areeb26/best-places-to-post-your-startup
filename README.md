# Best Places to Post Your Startup

A curated list of reputable websites where founders can launch, list or share a startup. Every entry was checked for real moderation, a real audience and a working site. Link farms, badge-swap directories and sites that sell backlinks are left out on purpose.

Entries show the pricing model (**Free**, **Freemium** or **Paid**), a one-line note, and the link type (dofollow or nofollow) where it is known. Pricing and policies change, so check each site before you submit.

## How we chose

Each site was reviewed against the same quality filter before it was added:

- **Reputation and moderation.** The site reviews submissions (by hand or through clear community rules) and has a real audience of users, buyers or readers.
- **Clean link profile.** Sites that sell "DR" or backlinks, run link-exchange or badge-swap schemes (a badge on your site is required to get a free listing), or show signs of spam were excluded.
- **Safe content.** Sites listing casino, gambling or adult content, or carrying injected spam links, were excluded.
- **Alive and reachable.** Every included URL was re-checked with an HTTP request that follows redirects. Dead, parked and 404 pages were removed. A few well-known sites block automated requests (HTTP 403 or a bot check); they were kept because they are widely used and open normally in a browser.
- **One entry per domain.** Sister sites that share one vendor portal (for example the regional Capterra sites) are noted under a single entry.

"Dofollow" or "Nofollow" is shown only where it is known from our review; otherwise it is left out. Approval waits are approximate and based on what each site states.

## Launch platforms

Product launch sites and curated startup showcases.

- [Awesome Indie](https://awesomeindie.com) - **Free**. Indie product launch community with daily rankings. Sign in with Google, GitHub or X. [Submit](https://awesomeindie.com/submit) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Beta Bound](https://betabound.com) - **Free**. Beta-tester community run by Centercode; request a free announcement of a beta test. Not every request is posted. [Submit](https://betabound.com/announce/)
- [BetaList](https://betalist.com) - **Freemium**. Moderated showcase for early-stage and pre-launch startups. The free queue can take weeks to months; paid option to expedite. [Submit](https://betalist.com/submit) _Links: Nofollow._
- [DevHunt](https://devhunt.org) - **Freemium**. Launch platform for developer tools with community voting.
- [Launching Next](https://www.launchingnext.com) - **Freemium**. Long-running startup directory. The free queue is manual and can take months; paid option to expedite. [Submit](https://www.launchingnext.com/submit/)
- [Microlaunch](https://microlaunch.net) - **Freemium**. Launch platform where products run month-long launches.
- [Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io) - **Free**. Weekly, moderated launchpad on the Peerlist professional network. Requires a personal Peerlist profile. [Submit](https://peerlist.io/launchpad)
- [Pitchwall](https://pitchwall.co) - **Freemium**. Product launch directory (formerly BetaPage). Free listings are approved manually in about 30 days; paid option to skip the wait. [Submit](https://pitchwall.co/submit)
- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com) - **Free**. The largest moderated launch platform. Plan a launch day; a hunter is optional. [Submit](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Startup Inspire](https://www.startupinspire.com) - **Freemium**. Startup directory and gallery with manual review; paid tiers available. [Submit](https://www.startupinspire.com/submit)
- [StartupBase](https://startupbase.io) - **Free**. Startup launch directory with community upvotes. Sign-in is via social login. [Submit](https://startupbase.io/submit)
- [Startups.fyi](https://www.startups.fyi) - **Freemium**. Curated directory and newsletter of profitable startups.
- [startups.gallery](https://startups.gallery) - **Free**. Hand-picked gallery of early-stage startups; every submission is reviewed by hand. No account needed. [Submit](https://tally.so/r/3ylOB8)
- [Tiny Startups](https://www.tinystartups.com) - **Freemium**. Human-curated launch platform and newsletter for small startups. [Submit](https://www.tinystartups.com/submit)
- [Uneed](https://www.uneed.best) - **Freemium**. Curated daily launch platform. Free listings join a waiting line; paid option to skip it. [Submit](https://www.uneed.best/submit-a-tool) _Links: Dofollow only on paid or badge listings._

## Startup databases

Company and startup databases used by investors, journalists and partners.

- [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com) - **Free**. Widely used company database; create or claim a company profile. _Links: Nofollow._
- [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co) - **Free**. Startup and venture-capital database; companies can claim and update their profile. [Submit](https://app.dealroom.co/) _Links: Nofollow._
- [F6S](https://www.f6s.com) - **Free**. Large startup platform for company profiles, accelerators, deals and programs.
- [Gust](https://gust.com) - **Free**. Startup and investor platform; free company profile visible to investors and accelerators. [Submit](https://gust.com/users/sign_up)
- [MAGNiTT](https://magnitt.com) - **Free**. Startup and venture database focused on the Middle East, Africa, Pakistan and Turkey.
- [Owler](https://www.owler.com) - **Free**. Company-data platform; companies can claim and edit their profile. _Links: Nofollow._
- [Startup Ranking](https://www.startupranking.com) - **Free**. Long-running startup ranking directory. Sign-in is via social login. [Submit](https://www.startupranking.com/login)
- [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com) - **Free**. Startup and private-company database. Profiles are created by Tracxn; founders can claim them. _Links: Nofollow._
- [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com) - **Free**. Startup company profiles and jobs (formerly AngelList Talent). _Links: Nofollow._

## SaaS and software review sites

Software directories, alternatives sites and B2B review platforms.

- [Alternative.me](https://alternative.me) - **Free**. Long-running software alternatives directory; submit from the dashboard after creating an account. [Submit](https://alternative.me/how-to/submit-software/)
- [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net) - **Free**. Large, moderated software-alternatives site. New listings go through a review queue. [Submit](https://alternativeto.net/add-app/) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com) - **Free**. Gartner Digital Markets review site. One vendor account also covers GetApp, Software Advice and regional Capterra sites; pay-per-click is optional. [Submit](https://www.capterra.com/vendors/sign-up) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Crozdesk](https://crozdesk.com) - **Free**. Software search and review site; vendor listings are approved manually through its Revleads vendor portal. [Submit](https://vendor.revleads.com/)
- [Featured Customers](https://www.featuredcustomers.com) - **Freemium**. B2B customer-reference site for case studies and testimonials.
- [G2](https://www.g2.com) - **Free**. Leading B2B software review platform; free basic profile (a business email is expected). [Submit](https://sell.g2.com/create-a-profile) _Links: Nofollow._
- [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com) - **Free**. Gartner Digital Markets review site; uses the same vendor portal as Capterra. [Submit](https://www.capterra.com/vendors/sign-up) _Links: Nofollow._
- [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com) - **Freemium**. Moderated software-alternatives directory. The free queue can take 1-12 weeks; paid fast-track available. [Submit](https://www.saashub.com/submit) _Links: Dofollow for verified listings._
- [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com) - **Freemium**. Large software review and comparison directory; vendors manage their own listing.
- [Slashdot Software](https://slashdot.org/software/) - **Free**. Slashdot Media software directory; uses the same vendor portal as SourceForge. [Submit](https://sourceforge.net/software/vendors/) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Softpedia](https://www.softpedia.com) - **Free**. Long-established software download library; best suited to downloadable apps.
- [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com) - **Free**. Gartner Digital Markets review and advisory site; uses the same vendor portal as Capterra. [Submit](https://www.capterra.com/vendors/sign-up) _Links: Nofollow._
- [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com) - **Free**. Large B2B software review site with strong reach in India and the Middle East. [Submit](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/vendors)
- [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net) - **Free**. Long-established software directory with a free commercial listing via its vendor portal. [Submit](https://sourceforge.net/software/vendors/) _Links: Nofollow._
- [StackShare](https://stackshare.io) - **Free**. Directory of developer tools and company tech stacks. Sign-in is via GitHub or Google. _Links: Nofollow._
- [TrustRadius](https://www.trustradius.com) - **Free**. Moderated B2B software review site with a free vendor profile. [Submit](https://www.trustradius.com/vendors) _Links: Nofollow._
- [WebCatalog](https://webcatalog.io) - **Free**. Catalog of web apps with real users; new apps are reviewed.

## Communities

Communities and publications where founders can share what they built.

- [Changelog News](https://changelog.com) - **Free**. Developer news from The Changelog. Sign in to submit; your own work is allowed, tutorials are not. [Submit](https://changelog.com/news/submit)
- [DEV Community](https://dev.to) - **Free**. Large developer community; write a genuine post about what you built. [Submit](https://dev.to/new) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Hacker News (Show HN)](https://news.ycombinator.com) - **Free**. Heavily moderated tech community. Show HN posts must be something people can try; no marketing tone. [Submit](https://news.ycombinator.com/submit) _Links: Nofollow._
- [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com) - **Free**. Tech publication where stories are editorially reviewed before publishing. [Submit](https://app.hackernoon.com/new) _Links: Dofollow (canonical link)._
- [Hashnode](https://hashnode.com) - **Free**. Developer blogging platform and community. _Links: Nofollow._
- [Inc42](https://inc42.com) - **Free**. Indian startup media; submit a startup for potential editorial coverage. [Submit](https://inc42.com/startup-submission/)
- [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com) - **Free**. Moderated founder community; add a product page and share progress posts. [Submit](https://www.indiehackers.com/products) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Land-book](https://land-book.com) - **Freemium**. Curated landing-page design gallery; showcase a well-designed site. Submission needs an account. [Guidelines](https://land-book.com/guidelines)
- [Netted](https://www.netted.net) - **Free**. Editorial newsletter from The Webby Awards covering useful sites and apps; pitch by email (see contact page). [Contact](https://www.netted.net/contact-us/)
- [r/SaaS](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaaS/) - **Free**. Reddit community for SaaS founders. Read and follow the subreddit's self-promotion rules. _Links: Nofollow._
- [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com) - **Free**. Founder-interview publication; apply to share how you started and grew. Requires sign-in. [Submit](https://www.starterstory.com/share)
- [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com) - **Free**. Startup media for the Middle East and North Africa; pitch a story to the editors.

## Business listings

Official and established business listing services.

- [Apple Business Connect](https://businessconnect.apple.com) - **Free**. Official Apple listing for Maps, Wallet and Siri. Requires an Apple ID.
- [Bing Places](https://www.bingplaces.com) - **Free**. Official Microsoft business listing; can import from Google Business Profile.
- [Clutch](https://clutch.co) - **Free**. Moderated B2B services directory with verified reviews; best for agencies and service companies. [Submit](https://clutch.co/get-listed) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Foursquare for Business](https://foursquare.com) - **Free**. Location-data platform; claim or add a business listing. _Links: Nofollow._
- [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/) - **Free**. Official Google listing for Search and Maps. Verification (for example by postcard or video) is required. [Submit](https://business.google.com/create)
- [The Manifest](https://themanifest.com) - **Free**. Clutch-owned business directory; profiles are created through Clutch. [Submit](https://clutch.co/get-listed) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com) - **Freemium**. Major consumer review platform with a free business profile. [Submit](https://signup.business.trustpilot.com/create-account) _Links: Nofollow._
- [Yell](https://www.yell.com) - **Freemium**. Major UK business directory; free listing requires a UK address. [Submit](https://www.yell.com/free-listing/)
- [Yelp for Business](https://biz.yelp.com) - **Free**. Official Yelp business listing, mainly for US businesses. _Links: Nofollow._

- [Discover Cloud](https://www.discovercloud.com) - **Free**. AppDirect B2B cloud software marketplace; registering a product is free. [Submit](https://www.discovercloud.com/become-a-vendor)

- [SideProjectors](https://www.sideprojectors.com) - **Free**. Marketplace to showcase or sell side projects.
- [Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market) - **Free**. Official Wix app marketplace; requires a Wix app. [Submit](https://dev.wix.com/) _Links: Nofollow._
